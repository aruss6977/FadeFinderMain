# How to Use FadeFinder

---

## 1. Getting Started (Account Creation & Sign In)
1. **Launch the App:** Open FadeFinder to arrive at the **Welcome Screen**.
2. **Create an Account:**
   * Tap **Create Account** to register as a new fighter.
   * Enter your email, username, and a strong password (matching confirmation required).
   * Fill out your **Fighter Profile** details—including your name, age, height, primary fighting style (`Boxing`, `Muay Thai`, `BJJ`, `MMA`, or `Wrestling`), bio, and current weight in pounds (`lbs`).
   * FadeFinder automatically assigns you to your official weight division (e.g., Lightweight, Welterweight, Middleweight) based on the weight you enter.
3. **Sign In:** Returning users can tap **Sign In** on the Welcome Screen and log in with their credentials.

---

## 2. Main Navigation Bar
Once signed in, a persistent bottom navigation bar lets you switch between the four core sections of the app at any time:
* **Discover** (Compass icon) — Browse and challenge fighters in your weight class.
* **Vote** (Ballot icon) — View confirmed community matchups and cast votes.
* **Chats** (Speech bubble icon) — Message matched fighters and lock in bout details.
* **Profile** (Person icon) — View your fighter card, manage active challenges, or edit your profile.

---

## 3. Finding & Challenging Fighters (`Discover`)
1. **Check Your Division:** At the top of the Discover screen, you will see your active weight division (e.g., `WELTERWEIGHT FIGHTERS`). Only fighters in your weight class appear in your deck.
2. **Filter by Style:** Tap any of the style chips near the top (`All styles`, `Boxing`, `Muay Thai`, `BJJ`, `MMA`, `Wrestling`) to narrow down opponents by discipline.
3. **Pass or Challenge:**
   * Tap the **Pass (X)** button or swipe left to skip a fighter.
   * Tap the **Challenge / Match** button or swipe right to send a sparring challenge.
4. **Undo a Swipe:** If you accidentally passed or challenged someone, tap **Undo last swipe** at the bottom of the screen to bring their card back.
5. **Refresh Deck:** Tap the refresh icon in the top-right corner to reload available fighters from the database.

---

## 4. Coordinating a Sparring Session (`Chats`)
1. Tap the **Chats** tab in the bottom navigation bar to see fighters you have matched with or challenged.
2. Tap a fighter's thread to open the **Conversation Screen**.
3. Type a message in the text box at the bottom and tap **Send** to coordinate a gym, date, and sparring rules.
4. Once both fighters agree, use the bout confirmation option in the chat to lock in the matchup and send it to the Arena Voting board.

---

## 5. Voting on Matchups (`Vote`)
1. Tap the **Vote** tab to browse confirmed sparring matchups.
2. Review both fighters' names, weight division, and fighting styles on each matchup card.
3. Tap the fighter you think will win to cast your vote—the live percentage tally bar updates immediately.

---

## 6. Managing Your Profile & Weight Class (`Profile`)
1. Tap the **Profile** tab to view your personal fighter card and stats.
2. **Manage Active Challenges:** Scroll down to **Active Challenges** to review pending sparring requests or withdraw a challenge.
3. **Edit Profile & Weight Class:**
   * Tap **Edit Profile** to update your bio, fighting style, weight, or password.
   * If you enter a new weight that moves you into a different weight division and save your changes, FadeFinder automatically updates your division and refreshes the **Discover** tab with fighters in your new weight class.
4. **Sign Out:** Tap **Sign Out** on the Profile screen to safely end your session and return to the Welcome Screen.
